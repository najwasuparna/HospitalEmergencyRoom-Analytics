# Hospital Emergency Room Operational Analytics

A comprehensive **multi-page Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze emergency room patient demographics, admission trends, service efficiency, and operational performance.

---

## Dashboard Overview & Preview

### 1. Executive Summary & Demographics
* **Key Focus:** Executive KPI summary (Total Patients, Avg Wait Time, Avg Satisfaction Score), department referral distribution, and patient demographics (Gender & Race breakdown).
* **Preview:**
![Executive Summary](./Executive%20Summary%20&%20Demographics.png)

### 2. Hospital Operations & Service Performance
* **Focus:** Detailed operational analysis including admission status (`Admission Flag`) per department, average wait times vs. satisfaction scores matrix, and patient admission trends over time.
* **Preview:**
![Operations Performance](./Hospital%20Operations%20&%20Service%20Performance.png)

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, measure aggregation, and multi-page dashboard layout.
* **Data Visualization Principles:** Executive card positioning, thematic coloring, and clear section headers.
